SECTION 8: SETTLEMENT & CASH REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

8.1 T+1 SETTLEMENT RULE
Customer understands:
- Settlement Date = Trade Date + 1 business day
- Settlement.executedDate + 1 business day = Settlement.settlementDate
- Settlement occurs automatically through clearing house
- Customer responsible for ensuring funds available for settlement

8.2 NO TRADING WITH UNSETTLED FUNDS
- Funds from sales NOT available until settlement complete
- Example:
    * Monday: Sell 100 shares of ABC for $5,000
    * Funds settle Tuesday (T+1)
    * Can only use $5,000 on Tuesday or later
    * Using funds Monday = violation

- Company enforces this through:
    * Account.availableForTrading tracks only settled funds
    * Unsettled funds shown separately
    * Orders rejected if insufficient settled cash

8.3 GOOD FAITH VIOLATION
- Trading with unsettled funds = "Good Faith Violation"
- First violation: WARNING
- Second violation within 12 months: TRADING RESTRICTED for 90 days
- Third violation: ACCOUNT CLOSED
- Customer responsible for tracking settled vs unsettled funds

8.4 SUFFICIENT CASH REQUIREMENT
Before submitting BUY order:
- Account.cashBalance must be ≥ (Order.quantity × Order.price)
- Company verifies sufficient funds before order execution
- Order REJECTED if insufficient cash

After order execution:
- Account.cashBalance must remain ≥ $0
- Account.cashBalance must remain ≥ $100 minimum

8.5 SETTLEMENT FAILURES
If settlement fails:
- Order status remains "EXECUTED"
- Company will notify Customer immediately
- Customer must deposit funds to cover settlement
- Failure to settle within 5 days = account suspension
- Company may force position liquidation
